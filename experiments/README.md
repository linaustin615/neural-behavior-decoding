# Historical experiment archive

The 54 dated folders preserve the original experiment code, protocols, reports, configurations, histories, small results, logs and figures. They include unsuccessful tests, feasibility reviews, syntheses that reuse fits, and one reconstruction study stopped before evaluation. Folder counts and result-file counts are not independent experiments or newly trained models.

Start with the [experiment index](../research/EXPERIMENTS.md) and [complete file inventory](../research/archive_inventory.csv). All archived sources and reports are preserved byte-for-byte; later cleanup does not rewrite earlier conclusions or proposals. Historical status statements and next-step suggestions describe their time of writing. The [root README](../README.md) states the final benchmark interpretation.

Raw recordings, prepared arrays, trained checkpoints and most per-run prediction arrays remain local and are omitted from Git. References to these paths and original workstation paths remain as provenance, not promises that the files are included. Some scripts depend on omitted inputs, original paths, and earlier study modules. Do not launch an old pipeline merely to inspect its results: it may start downloads or training.

Use [the clean core](../docs/CODE_TOUR.md) for new independent fits and [the compact saved predictions](../portfolio/REPRODUCIBILITY.md) to reproduce the published findings. Completed diagnostics and their recorded outcomes do not need to be rerun for that purpose.
